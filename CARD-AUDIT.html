Torekh — card fact-check audit

Checked 23 October 2026. Official register updated 22 October 2026.

Audit of the content currently displayed is complete. Research coverage remains partial.

37 active identities and ballot-letter combinations, 109 displayed candidate names, 37 party-submission descriptions, 27 proposal summaries across nine lists, and every poll badge were checked. Five policy bullets were clarified. This does not certify every party platform, political record, translation or outcome.

What the evidence supports

  • All 37 active names and letters match the official register. All displayed candidate names match their official profile order. Blue and White (כן) is excluded as withdrawn.
  • The 27 summaries match party primary sources after the clarifications below. They describe proposals, not verified delivery.
  • The N12/Midgam poll supplies 11 seat figures totaling 120, plus four explicitly below-threshold shares. The other 22 lists are marked unreported, not zero.
  • Actions and outcomes contain incomplete-review notices, not factual performance claims.

Clarifications applied to the cards

  1. Beyahad: childcare summary now includes the eligibility condition tied to parents’ earning capacity.
  2. Beyahad: 50% target is specifically senior civil-service posts and government-company boards.
  3. Yashar: service proposal now explicitly mentions the IDF as the primary framework.
  4. Yisrael Beiteinu: service summary now includes the proposal’s distinct sanctions for evasion and desertion; competition/regulation is no longer bundled into that bullet.
  5. Amcha Israel: international agreements are a proposed route for the emigration policy; wording no longer risks implying agreements already exist.

Limits that remain

  • Source matching establishes what a party states, not whether proposals are feasible, legal, funded, delivered or effective.
  • Seven of the nine policy sources lack a clear publication date; date of access does not establish the date the policy was adopted. Democrats document is dated April 2026; Joint List platform explicitly identifies the 2026 election.
  • Coverage is uneven: 28 lists have no reviewed policy summary. Blank tabs must not be treated as absence of policy or performance.
  • Poll is one historical N12/Midgam snapshot, not a multi-poll forecast or election result. 0 is an inference from an explicitly reported below-threshold share. The 22 omitted lists cannot be assigned zero.
  • No independent voting-record, implementation, budget-execution or causal-outcome review has been completed.
  • The official register calls list ק “תקומה”; its profile heading says “דרך חזון ותקומה”. Card retains the register name; this is a source naming variation, not a ballot-letter mismatch.
  • Hebrew, Arabic and English wording checked for meaning by this audit; independent fluent-editor review is still pending. Russian and Amharic policy text uses explicitly labelled English fallback.
  • Letters verified against the official register; exact printed ballot artwork/PDF poster not visually verified. Cards are labelled identification illustrations.
