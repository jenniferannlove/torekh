# Contributing to Torekh

Please propose corrections through an issue or pull request, with the affected list, the current text, a proposed correction, a direct source URL, the source date if available, and the date checked. Explain whether the evidence is a party proposal, an action or a measured outcome.

Preserve official Hebrew names and ballot letters. Keep summaries neutral and readable on a phone. Do not rank parties or recommend a vote. Missing evidence should remain explicit. Do not infer an individual's religion from a party identity badge.

For coalition statements, identify the speaker, target, direction, date, scope and conditions. Distinguish refusing a person from refusing a party, nomination from government membership and outside support. A mathematical majority is not an agreed coalition. Unreported seats are unknown, not zero.

For translations, identify the language and source text; seek fluent editorial review. Do not silently replace official ballot letters with transliterations. Test the page at a narrow phone width and in right-to-left languages after interface changes.

AI assistance is welcome when disclosed, but is not a source or a substitute for checking the cited evidence. Keep secrets, personal phone numbers, account exports and private discussions out of contributions.

By submitting original contributions, you agree to license original code under MIT and original editorial content under CC BY 4.0, as described in LICENSING.md. Submit only material you have the right to contribute and identify third-party material and its terms. No transfer of copyright ownership is required.
